Confidence tags used below:
- **[MULTI]**: confirmed by two or more independent sources.
- **[SINGLE]**: one source only.
- **[UNCERTAIN]**: low-reliability source, a jokey or retold account, or sources that conflict.

## 1. Premise, story and characters

### Takeaway
The villain, the Master of Mischief, uses a weather machine to freeze Treasure Mountain in snow and ice and scatters the treasures from the castle at the summit. The player, an anonymous "Super Seeker", climbs the mountain, recovers the treasures and returns them to the castle's treasure chest so the mountain thaws. A frozen crown is the symbol of what is being restored. The cast is a guide bird (Chirp), many helpful elves who give out the math problems, shopkeepers, a gatekeeper Yeti, a scale-room character, and roaming "Snowbullies" that act as hazards.

### Cited Findings
- **Premise [MULTI]:** The Master of Mischief uses a weather machine to freeze Treasure Mountain in snow and ice and scatter the castle's treasures. The player, as the Super Seeker, must return the treasures to the castle chest to thaw the mountain. Sources: [Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Treasure_MathStorm!); [Archive.org TMS_VGA description](https://archive.org/details/TMS_VGA); [sk8nbike (former TLC artist)](https://sk8nbike.com/Treasure_Mathstorm_01.htm).
- **The king and the crown [UNCERTAIN, sources differ on framing]:**
  - The Archive.org description says the "king will have his power back" and all the ice melts once every treasure is restored ([Archive.org TMS_VGA](https://archive.org/details/TMS_VGA); [Archive.org treasure-mathstorm](https://archive.org/details/treasure-mathstorm)).
  - The Potsdam review describes the goal as collecting treasures "to unfreeze a crown" that was frozen in a snowstorm ([Potsdam review](https://people.potsdam.edu/betrusak/reviews/readerrabbittreasuremath.html)).
  - The Vizzed ending says "the crown melts" ([Vizzed](https://www.vizzed.com/boards/thread.php?id=105443)).
  - Conclusion: a crown is confirmed by two sources. Whether it belongs to a king or to the villain is unclear.
- **Villain's motive [UNCERTAIN]:** The LP's comic retelling says the Master of Mischief is sulking after earlier defeats by the Super Solvers, and that his weather machine makes things colder and "messes with clocks" ([LP Update 14](https://lparchive.org/Super-Solvers-Series/Update%2014/)). The clock detail may be invented for the joke.
- **Player character [MULTI]:** The player is the "Super Seeker", the sixth Super Seekers/Super Solvers title ([Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Treasure_MathStorm!)). The artist says the character was deliberately drawn in a snowsuit and scarf that hide identifying features, so any child can imagine being the hero ([sk8nbike](https://sk8nbike.com/Treasure_Mathstorm_01.htm)). The character carries a backpack and a butterfly-style net ([sk8nbike](https://sk8nbike.com/Treasure_Mathstorm_01.htm)). The player types in their name at the start ([Vizzed](https://www.vizzed.com/boards/thread.php?id=105443)).
- **Chirp [MULTI]:** Chirp is a snow bird and the tutorial and hint guide. Chirp hints at the start (movement, catching elves), when the player has no coins, nets or snowballs, when a Snowbully hits the player, when the player idles a few seconds on first entering a math room, and when the player pays the wrong amount at the store. Sources: [Free Game Empire hint strings](https://www.freegameempire.com/games/Super-Solvers-Treasure-MathStorm/manual); [Vizzed](https://www.vizzed.com/boards/thread.php?id=105443). The LP jokingly calls Chirp a dove ([LP 14](https://lparchive.org/Super-Solvers-Series/Update%2014/)).
- **Elves [MULTI]:** Elves wander the slopes. The player catches them with the net, and each caught elf asks a math question. Elves carrying a **scroll** give the "special number" needed to uncover treasure. Elves also pay coins. Sources: [Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Treasure_MathStorm!); [Free Game Empire](https://www.freegameempire.com/games/Super-Solvers-Treasure-MathStorm/manual); [Vizzed](https://www.vizzed.com/boards/thread.php?id=105443). Two further roles:
  - In the CD version, elves explain each station's task out loud, with the text also on screen. They repeat the directions until the child clicks to begin ([Potsdam](https://people.potsdam.edu/betrusak/reviews/readerrabbittreasuremath.html)).
  - Elves move unpredictably, so they can appear at any time ([Potsdam](https://people.potsdam.edu/betrusak/reviews/readerrabbittreasuremath.html)).
- **Elves as helpers inside the math rooms [MULTI]:**
  - Time Igloo: an elf wears a watch and taps his foot while the player works. He shakes his head sadly at a wrong answer ([LP 14](https://lparchive.org/Super-Solvers-Series/Update%2014/)). Vizzed calls this character the "time master" ([Vizzed](https://www.vizzed.com/boards/thread.php?id=105443)).
  - Castle: an elf takes the treasures and hands over the end-of-climb prize ([LP 15](https://lparchive.org/Super-Solvers-Series/Update%2015/); [Vizzed](https://www.vizzed.com/boards/thread.php?id=105443)).
- **"Equality" [SINGLE]:** The Gold Room character who holds the balance weights is named Equality ([LP 15](https://lparchive.org/Super-Solvers-Series/Update%2015/)).
- **Shopkeepers [MULTI]:** Each level has a store whose keeper sells nets and tools and makes change ([Free Game Empire](https://www.freegameempire.com/games/Super-Solvers-Treasure-MathStorm/manual)). Each level has a **different** shopkeeper: the second is an old man and the third a woman ([LP 16](https://lparchive.org/Super-Solvers-Series/Update%2016/)).
- **Yeti [MULTI]:** A large Yeti guards the door to the summit castle. He will not let the player in until they have the ladders, and then he sets a missing-number or pattern puzzle ([Vizzed](https://www.vizzed.com/boards/thread.php?id=105443); [LP 15](https://lparchive.org/Super-Solvers-Series/Update%2015/)). On a correct answer he honks his nose and lets the player in ([LP 15](https://lparchive.org/Super-Solvers-Series/Update%2015/)).
- **Snowbullies [MULTI]:** Snowbullies are animated, floating snowballs that roam a level at higher ranks. They knock the player back and steal money ([Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Treasure_MathStorm!); [Free Game Empire](https://www.freegameempire.com/games/Super-Solvers-Treasure-MathStorm/manual); [Vizzed](https://www.vizzed.com/boards/thread.php?id=105443); [LP 16](https://lparchive.org/Super-Solvers-Series/Update%2016/)).
- **Ending scene [SINGLE, Mac version]:**
  - The final ending comes after all the treasure has been collected.
  - The player reaches the summit, where the Master of Mischief sits in his chair. The crown melts, and when he tries to restart his snow machine it malfunctions. The player does not fight him.
  - Depending on the version, he ends up frozen or blown over with snow, and Treasure Mountain returns to spring or summer.
  - Source: [Vizzed](https://www.vizzed.com/boards/thread.php?id=105443). The LP author calls the ending underwhelming and says it has no sound ([LP 16](https://lparchive.org/Super-Solvers-Series/Update%2016/)). The artist confirms that summer and winter versions of the mountain art were made ([sk8nbike](https://sk8nbike.com/Treasure_Mathstorm_01.htm)).

### Inferences
- The story is a thin frame: "a villain froze the land, return the treasures to undo it". Almost all narrative weight sits in the opening animation and the final ending. In between, the "story" is the visible progress of climbing higher.
- The cast splits cleanly by job:
  - Guide (Chirp)
  - Problem-givers who reward (elves, the time elf, Equality, the shopkeepers)
  - Gatekeeper (the Yeti)
  - Mild hazard (Snowbullies)
  - Off-screen antagonist (the Master of Mischief)
- The villain is never fought. He is defeated as a side effect of the player's good deeds. This structure suits a young child.
- For an original design, avoid TLC-specific names: Super Seeker, Master of Mischief, Chirp, Snowbullies, Equality, Treasure Mountain.

### Gaps
- No primary source (manual or intro script) was found for the exact opening cutscene. The only intro retellings are the jokey LP and the generic box text.
- It is unclear who owns the castle and crown (a king, or the Master of Mischief, who is shown occupying the castle). Sources conflict or are vague.
- I could not confirm whether the Master of Mischief appears on screen during ordinary climbs or only in the final ending. LP 15 says he is shown "relaxing" in the castle room during a normal deposit, so he may appear every time. [UNCERTAIN]

## 2. Setting and map structure

### Takeaway
The game takes place on one snowy mountain split into **three levels** stacked vertically, with a **clubhouse** at the bottom and a **castle** at the top. Each level is a left-right scrolling slope with roaming elves, snowball piles, a shop, and one math building that produces that level's climbing tool. An obstacle at the right or top end needs **4** of the level's tool to pass:
- Level 1: a cliff, which needs 4 picks from the Time Igloo.
- Level 2: a frozen fountain, which needs 4 catapult parts from the Gold Room.
- Level 3: the Icy Gate, which needs 4 ladders from the Crystal Cave, then the Yeti's puzzle.

After the treasures are deposited in the castle, the player drops back to the clubhouse and the whole climb repeats at a higher rank.

### Cited Findings
- **Three levels, gated by climbing supplies [MULTI]:** The mountain has three levels, and the player cannot go higher until they have climbing supplies such as ice axes, ladders or catapult parts ([Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Treasure_MathStorm!); [sk8nbike](https://sk8nbike.com/Treasure_Mathstorm_01.htm); [Vizzed](https://www.vizzed.com/boards/thread.php?id=105443)).
- **The three gates, from the in-game messages [SINGLE primary, corroborated in part]:**
  - Climbing area: 4 picks, from the Time Igloo or the store.
  - Frozen fountain: 4 catapult parts to jump to the next level, from the Gold Room or the store.
  - Icy Gate: 4 ladders to climb to the castle, from the Crystal Cave or the store.
  - Source: [Free Game Empire](https://www.freegameempire.com/games/Super-Solvers-Treasure-MathStorm/manual).
  - Corroboration: the "4 picks from the Time Igloo" and "ladder parts from the Crystal Cave, needed for the Yeti door" are confirmed by [Vizzed](https://www.vizzed.com/boards/thread.php?id=105443). Catapult parts from balancing scales are confirmed by [Vizzed](https://www.vizzed.com/boards/thread.php?id=105443) and [LP 15](https://lparchive.org/Super-Solvers-Series/Update%2015/).
- **Level 1 obstacle [MULTI]:** The four parts let the player scale a wall or cliff where a giant tree once stood ([LP 14](https://lparchive.org/Super-Solvers-Series/Update%2014/)). The artist says the mountain was drawn with obvious footholds for the ice picks, and that a tree with ladder boards was considered and dropped because it was winter ([sk8nbike](https://sk8nbike.com/Treasure_Mathstorm_01.htm)).
- **Level 2 obstacle [MULTI]:** The catapult launches the player up from where a water fountain used to be, and the landing is a rough tumble ([LP 15](https://lparchive.org/Super-Solvers-Series/Update%2015/)). The in-game strings call this spot the "frozen fountain" ([Free Game Empire](https://www.freegameempire.com/games/Super-Solvers-Treasure-MathStorm/manual)).
- **Level 3 and the castle [MULTI]:**
  - The Yeti guards the golden door to the castle and needs 4 ladder parts plus a solved missing-number puzzle ([Vizzed](https://www.vizzed.com/boards/thread.php?id=105443); [LP 15](https://lparchive.org/Super-Solvers-Series/Update%2015/)).
  - Inside the castle, the player picks one of several ladders. At the LP's difficulty only one was correct ([LP 15](https://lparchive.org/Super-Solvers-Series/Update%2015/)).
  - The player climbs to a large chest and deposits the treasure ([Vizzed](https://www.vizzed.com/boards/thread.php?id=105443)).
- **Return trip [MULTI]:**
  - After the deposit, an elf hands over a present or prize. In the LP the present turns into a parachute, and the player floats down to land outside the clubhouse, while the present drifts into the prize room ([LP 15](https://lparchive.org/Super-Solvers-Series/Update%2015/)).
  - In the Mac version, the player jumps out of a window and returns to the mountain base, where the rewards are shown in the treasure room ([Vizzed](https://www.vizzed.com/boards/thread.php?id=105443)).
  - Wikipedia: the player can then start again from the bottom ([Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Treasure_MathStorm!)).
- **Clubhouse and home base [MULTI]:** The game starts at the clubhouse ([sk8nbike](https://sk8nbike.com/Treasure_Mathstorm_01.htm)). The clubhouse displays prizes, and the number of prizes shows how many times the player has climbed ([Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Treasure_MathStorm!)). Rank-up messages appear in the clubhouse ([Free Game Empire](https://www.freegameempire.com/games/Super-Solvers-Treasure-MathStorm/manual)). The Mac walkthrough says play begins outside an igloo with a few nets ([Vizzed](https://www.vizzed.com/boards/thread.php?id=105443)).
- **Buildings on each level [MULTI]:**
  - A store on each level ([LP 16](https://lparchive.org/Super-Solvers-Series/Update%2016/); [Free Game Empire](https://www.freegameempire.com/games/Super-Solvers-Treasure-MathStorm/manual)).
  - The Time Igloo, a big igloo topped with a clock ([Vizzed](https://www.vizzed.com/boards/thread.php?id=105443)).
  - The Gold Room, also called the "scale store" ([Vizzed](https://www.vizzed.com/boards/thread.php?id=105443); [LP 15](https://lparchive.org/Super-Solvers-Series/Update%2015/)).
  - The Crystal Cave, also called the "gem cave" ([LP 15](https://lparchive.org/Super-Solvers-Series/Update%2015/); [Vizzed](https://www.vizzed.com/boards/thread.php?id=105443)).
  - The artist mentions an igloo "where games are played" and icy caves ([sk8nbike](https://sk8nbike.com/Treasure_Mathstorm_01.htm)).
- **Movement [MULTI]:** The player moves left and right around the mountain ([Free Game Empire](https://www.freegameempire.com/games/Super-Solvers-Treasure-MathStorm/manual); [ClassicReload DOS](https://classicreload.com/treasure-mathstorm.html)). There is no jumping, but the player can skate, which is faster ([LP 14](https://lparchive.org/Super-Solvers-Series/Update%2014/)). The artist notes the winter mountain has a narrower walking path than the summer art ([sk8nbike](https://sk8nbike.com/Treasure_Mathstorm_01.htm)).
- **Treasures per level [UNCERTAIN, sources conflict]:**
  - Vizzed (Mac): the first climbs hold 3 treasures per level, later climbs 4, then more again along with Snowbullies ([Vizzed](https://www.vizzed.com/boards/thread.php?id=105443)).
  - A search-engine snippet credited to an unnamed fan guide, possibly the GameFAQs guide (not verified directly): 2 treasures per area at the start, rising to 3 to 5 per area. It surfaced via a search result list including [GameFAQs](https://gamefaqs.gamespot.com/pc/930542-treasure-mathstorm/faqs/62898).
  - Wikipedia: higher ranks mean more treasures ([Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Treasure_MathStorm!)).

### Inferences
- Map structure as an ordered list for one climb (built from the sources above):
  1. Clubhouse / prize room (home)
  2. Level 1 slope: elves, snowball piles, store, Time Igloo, then the cliff (4 picks)
  3. Level 2 slope: elves, piles, store, Gold Room, then the frozen fountain (4 catapult parts)
  4. Level 3 slope: elves, piles, store, Crystal Cave, then the Icy Gate with the Yeti (4 ladders plus a pattern puzzle)
  5. Castle interior: choose the right ladder, deposit treasures in the chest, receive a prize
  6. Float or jump back down to the clubhouse; the prize appears in the room
- Each level pairs one math skill with one tool, which gives each level its own identity (time, then weight/equality, then place-value counting).
- The game seems to be strictly one-way within a climb: there is no mention of walking back down. This is not confirmed (see Gaps).

### Gaps
- I could not confirm whether every treasure on a level must be found before the gate can be passed, or whether the treasures can be left behind. Wikipedia's win condition is "deposit all treasures", which suggests all must be found, but the per-level rule is unknown.
- It is unclear whether the player can walk back down to an earlier level within a climb.
- The number of distinct screens per level is unknown, as is whether a level is a continuous scroll or a set of flip-screens.
- I could not confirm whether the "ice axes" (Wikipedia) and the "picks" (in-game strings) are the same item. They almost certainly are.

## 3. What the player does on each screen: problem triggers, and right vs. wrong answers

### Takeaway
There are two kinds of problem trigger.

1. **Out on the slopes:** the child walks around and swings a net at moving elves. A caught elf poses a problem, and solving it pays coins. A scroll-carrying elf instead reveals a **"special number"**. The child then goes to a snowball pile, adds or removes snowballs until the pile matches that number, and "checks" it to uncover a wrapped treasure.
2. **Inside buildings:** the child chooses to enter a math room (clock setting, balance scale, or crystal counting). Each correct answer earns one of 4 climbing parts, and once all 4 are earned, further correct answers pay coins.

Wrong answers have very soft consequences: a head-shake or a hint, never a loss of progress.

### Cited Findings
- **Catching elves [MULTI]:**
  - The player swings the net (spacebar) at elves. Each catch uses up a net ([Free Game Empire](https://www.freegameempire.com/games/Super-Solvers-Treasure-MathStorm/manual); [ClassicReload DOS](https://classicreload.com/treasure-mathstorm.html)).
  - Each caught elf poses a math problem. A correct answer tells the player how many snowballs to add or remove to uncover treasure, and the elf also hands over cents ([Vizzed](https://www.vizzed.com/boards/thread.php?id=105443)).
  - Answering elf questions can earn coins, hints, or the snowball count ([Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Treasure_MathStorm!); [Archive.org TMS_VGA](https://archive.org/details/TMS_VGA)).
- **Scroll elves and the "special number" [MULTI]:**
  - Wikipedia: the player catches an elf carrying a scroll, and a correct answer to its riddle reveals how many snowballs to place and where ([Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Treasure_MathStorm!)).
  - In-game hint: snowball piles need a special number, which the player learns by catching an elf with a scroll ([Free Game Empire](https://www.freegameempire.com/games/Super-Solvers-Treasure-MathStorm/manual)).
- **Snowball piles [MULTI]:**
  - The player sets a pile to the special number, then uses a separate "check" action to see whether treasure is underneath ([Free Game Empire](https://www.freegameempire.com/games/Super-Solvers-Treasure-MathStorm/manual)). Enter checks piles in the DOS version ([ClassicReload DOS](https://classicreload.com/treasure-mathstorm.html)).
  - The player carries snowballs and can run out. The hint for that case says to take more from another pile ([Free Game Empire](https://www.freegameempire.com/games/Super-Solvers-Treasure-MathStorm/manual)).
  - On success the snowballs blink and reveal a **wrapped** treasure, and the player cannot see which treasure it is at that point ([LP 14](https://lparchive.org/Super-Solvers-Series/Update%2014/)).
  - At higher difficulty the elves hide things more aggressively ([LP 14](https://lparchive.org/Super-Solvers-Series/Update%2014/)).
- **Time Igloo [MULTI]:**
  - Task types: read the time on a clock, or set an analog clock to match a digital time ([Vizzed](https://www.vizzed.com/boards/thread.php?id=105443)). The LP describes matching a digital clock to an analog one ([LP 14](https://lparchive.org/Super-Solvers-Series/Update%2014/)). The in-game help describes setting a digital clock to the right time from a clue ([Free Game Empire](https://www.freegameempire.com/games/Super-Solvers-Treasure-MathStorm/manual)).
  - Easier setting: the minute marks are numbered and the hour is highlighted in red. Harder problems ask for a time 15 minutes earlier or later than the one shown ([LP 14](https://lparchive.org/Super-Solvers-Series/Update%2014/)).
  - On a correct answer a spark flies from the elf's watch to the clock, and an elf hands over one of the four pieces. On a wrong answer the elf shakes his head sadly, with no other penalty described ([LP 14](https://lparchive.org/Super-Solvers-Series/Update%2014/)).
  - Each correct answer earns a pick (4 are needed). After that, extra problems earn cents ([Vizzed](https://www.vizzed.com/boards/thread.php?id=105443)).
- **Gold Room (balance scale) [MULTI]:**
  - The player balances a scale by moving gold weights until both sides are equal ([Free Game Empire](https://www.freegameempire.com/games/Super-Solvers-Treasure-MathStorm/manual); [LP 15](https://lparchive.org/Super-Solvers-Series/Update%2015/)). This earns catapult parts and then cents ([Vizzed](https://www.vizzed.com/boards/thread.php?id=105443)).
  - With the mouse, weights can be dragged onto the pans ([LP 15](https://lparchive.org/Super-Solvers-Series/Update%2015/)).
  - Difficulty rises by putting more weights on both sides, or by offering fewer weights to choose from (only three fit on a pan). There is also a "Mystery Bag" variant where the player works out an unknown weight. The LP author suggests guessing 5 first to halve the possibilities, which implies trial-and-error feedback such as higher/lower or a tipping scale ([LP 16](https://lparchive.org/Super-Solvers-Series/Update%2016/)).
- **Crystal Cave (counting by place value) [MULTI]:**
  - The player counts crystals and types the number ([Free Game Empire](https://www.freegameempire.com/games/Super-Solvers-Treasure-MathStorm/manual)). Each correct count earns a ladder part, and once all 4 are earned, further counts pay money ([Vizzed](https://www.vizzed.com/boards/thread.php?id=105443)).
  - Crystals come in single gems and in blocks of 10 and 100 ([LP 16](https://lparchive.org/Super-Solvers-Series/Update%2016/); [Free Game Empire](https://www.freegameempire.com/games/Super-Solvers-Treasure-MathStorm/manual)).
  - On a wrong answer, the game shows how many there are in the ones, tens and so on, so the player can correct the count ([LP 15](https://lparchive.org/Super-Solvers-Series/Update%2015/); [LP 16](https://lparchive.org/Super-Solvers-Series/Update%2016/)).
- **Store [MULTI]:**
  - The player pays the **exact** price by placing coins on the counter. Without exact change, the player can exchange a coin at the counter. A wrong amount triggers a Chirp hint ([Free Game Empire](https://www.freegameempire.com/games/Super-Solvers-Treasure-MathStorm/manual)).
  - Exact change is required, and prices go up with difficulty ([LP 14](https://lparchive.org/Super-Solvers-Series/Update%2014/)).
- **Yeti gate puzzle [MULTI]:** The Yeti asks for a missing number ([Vizzed](https://www.vizzed.com/boards/thread.php?id=105443)), described elsewhere as a pattern puzzle that gets harder at higher levels ([LP 15](https://lparchive.org/Super-Solvers-Series/Update%2015/)).
- **Castle ladder choice [SINGLE]:** Inside the castle the player picks one of several ladders, and at the LP's difficulty only one is correct ([LP 15](https://lparchive.org/Super-Solvers-Series/Update%2015/)). What decides the correct ladder is not explained.
- **Snowbully encounters [MULTI]:**
  - A Snowbully that hits the player takes 5 cents and moves on; it is not destroyed. Ducking while moving avoids them. The player is most exposed when standing to check piles or entering doors ([LP 16](https://lparchive.org/Super-Solvers-Series/Update%2016/)).
  - A well-timed net swing makes one dissipate, and with no nets left the player can only duck ([Vizzed](https://www.vizzed.com/boards/thread.php?id=105443)).
  - The in-game hint is to duck, or to catch one ([Free Game Empire](https://www.freegameempire.com/games/Super-Solvers-Treasure-MathStorm/manual)).
- **Problem content [MULTI]:**
  - The box or catalog text lists addition, subtraction, multiplication, telling time and counting money ([Archive.org TMS_VGA](https://archive.org/details/TMS_VGA)).
  - The reviewer lists addition, subtraction, counting money and change, and telling time ([Potsdam](https://people.potsdam.edu/betrusak/reviews/readerrabbittreasuremath.html)).
  - The reviewer adds that progress depends on answering a set number of questions in each skill area of a level, not on chance ([Potsdam](https://people.potsdam.edu/betrusak/reviews/readerrabbittreasuremath.html)).

### Inferences
Below is a reconstructed minute-by-minute loop for one level. It is inference built from the cited mechanics.
1. Walk or skate along the slope. Elves wander past.
2. Swing the net at an elf. One net is used up.
3. Answer the elf's problem.
   - Ordinary elf: the reward is coins.
   - Scroll elf: the reward is the special number.
4. Walk to a snowball pile. Take snowballs from it into the backpack, or add snowballs from the backpack, until the pile equals the special number. Press check.
5. If it is the right pile, a wrapped treasure appears and is collected. Otherwise, try another pile.
6. Enter the level's math building and answer problems until all 4 tool parts are earned. Optionally keep going to farm coins.
7. Visit the store to restock nets, paying exact change, or to buy tool parts outright as an alternative to the math room.
8. Walk to the gate. With 4 parts, the hero climbs, catapults or ladders up to the next level.

Other inferences:
- **Two interlocking economies.** Money buys nets, nets catch elves, and elves give money and numbers. Separately, math rooms give tools and tools open gates. The math rooms are also a money tap, which stops the child from getting stuck with no money and no nets. The game's own hint covers exactly that case.
- **Gentle feedback on wrong answers.** In the clock room, the only cost is a sad elf. In the crystal room, the game shows place-value feedback. The one real cost in the game is Snowbullies taking 5 cents, and it applies only at higher ranks.
- The store's exact-change rule turns ordinary shopping into a money-counting exercise without a separate mini-game.

### Gaps
- **What the scroll problems look like.** Their exact format is not documented. It is unclear whether they are plain arithmetic ("3 + 4"), word problems, or "make the pile 2 more than 5", and whether the scroll names a particular pile or position (Wikipedia says "and where") or any pile set to the number works.
- **Wrong pile or wrong number.** I could not confirm what happens when a pile is checked with the wrong count or at the wrong pile. It is probably just "nothing here".
- **Wrong answer to an elf.** It is unclear whether the elf runs off, retries, or whether the net is lost anyway.
- **The castle ladder choice.** The rule that decides which ladder is correct (likely a math check) is unknown.
- **The Mystery Bag.** Its exact feedback rules were not confirmed.

## 4. Items, tools, currency and collectibles

### Takeaway
- **Currency:** cents, as coins. In the UK version, pence.
- **Consumable:** nets, one used per catch.
- **Carried resource:** snowballs.
- **Climbing tools:** 4 picks, then 4 catapult parts, then 4 ladders, one set per level, each either earned in that level's math room or bought.
- **Information token:** the scroll's special number.
- **Main collectibles:** treasures, found wrapped under snowball piles and deposited in the castle chest.
- **Meta-collectible:** one prize toy per completed climb, shown in the clubhouse prize room. The toys animate when clicked.

### Cited Findings
- **Coins [MULTI]:**
  - Coins are earned by catching elves and by doing the math rooms, and spent at the store ([Free Game Empire](https://www.freegameempire.com/games/Super-Solvers-Treasure-MathStorm/manual); [Vizzed](https://www.vizzed.com/boards/thread.php?id=105443)).
  - The amounts are in cents ([Vizzed](https://www.vizzed.com/boards/thread.php?id=105443); [LP 16](https://lparchive.org/Super-Solvers-Series/Update%2016/)). The LP narrator paid for nets in pennies ([LP 14](https://lparchive.org/Super-Solvers-Series/Update%2014/)).
  - The British release uses pence ([Archive.org UK v1.1](https://archive.org/details/Treasure_MathStorm_1.1)).
- **Nets [MULTI]:** Nets are used up with each catch and bought at the store ([Free Game Empire](https://www.freegameempire.com/games/Super-Solvers-Treasure-MathStorm/manual)). The Vizzed author buys them about three at a time ([Vizzed](https://www.vizzed.com/boards/thread.php?id=105443)). The game starts the player with "a few" nets ([Vizzed](https://www.vizzed.com/boards/thread.php?id=105443)).
- **Snowballs [SINGLE primary]:** The player uses snowballs on piles and can run out, then must take more from another pile ([Free Game Empire](https://www.freegameempire.com/games/Super-Solvers-Treasure-MathStorm/manual)).
- **Picks, catapult parts and ladders [MULTI]:** 4 of each are needed. Each can be earned in the Time Igloo, the Gold Room or the Crystal Cave respectively, or bought in the store ([Free Game Empire](https://www.freegameempire.com/games/Super-Solvers-Treasure-MathStorm/manual); [Vizzed](https://www.vizzed.com/boards/thread.php?id=105443)). The store sells one set of level-advance parts per level ([LP 14](https://lparchive.org/Super-Solvers-Series/Update%2014/)).
- **Treasures [MULTI]:**
  - Treasures are found under snowball piles and returned to the castle chest ([Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Treasure_MathStorm!); [Vizzed](https://www.vizzed.com/boards/thread.php?id=105443)).
  - They are wrapped when found, so their identity is hidden ([LP 14](https://lparchive.org/Super-Solvers-Series/Update%2014/)).
- **Prizes (end-of-climb toys) [MULTI]:**
  - Each completed climb earns one prize, usually a children's toy such as a flute or toy train, shown in the clubhouse ([Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Treasure_MathStorm!)).
  - The prize ("grand treasure") is random ([Vizzed](https://www.vizzed.com/boards/thread.php?id=105443)) and can be a duplicate ([LP 14](https://lparchive.org/Super-Solvers-Series/Update%2014/)).
  - Clicking or playing with a toy makes it run a short animation, and some pairs of toys interact with each other ([LP 14](https://lparchive.org/Super-Solvers-Series/Update%2014/)).
  - The prize sequence matches the earlier game, Treasure Mountain ([LP 15](https://lparchive.org/Super-Solvers-Series/Update%2015/)).
- **Prices [MULTI, no numbers]:** Prices go up with difficulty ([LP 14](https://lparchive.org/Super-Solvers-Series/Update%2014/)). No source gave actual price figures ([Free Game Empire](https://www.freegameempire.com/games/Super-Solvers-Treasure-MathStorm/manual)).

### Inferences
- **Three layers of collectibles:** short-term tools (nets, snowballs), mid-term gate keys (4 parts per level), and long-term trophies (prize toys that build a visible collection in the home base).
- **The "earn it or buy it" choice for tool parts.** The child can pick between more practice in the math room and spending saved coins at the store. This gives agency and lets a child skip a skill area they find hard, at a coin cost.

### Gaps
- Exact prices for nets and tool parts at each rank are not known.
- Exact coin rewards per elf and per extra math-room answer are not known.
- The full prize list is not known. Only the flute and toy train are named.
- How many nets and how much money the player starts with are not known.
- Whether money and nets carry over between climbs is not known.

## 5. Win conditions, ranks, ending; timer, lives and failure state

### Takeaway
- **One climb is won** by passing all three gates and depositing every found treasure in the castle chest. That gives a prize and sends the player back to the clubhouse.
- **Rank:** deposited treasures raise the player's rank through star levels, from "Super Seeker" up to "Champion Super Seeker". Higher ranks bring more treasures per level, harder problems, higher prices, and Snowbullies.
- **The true ending** (the villain's machine breaking and the mountain thawing) plays after the final rank is completed.
- **No timer or lives** were found in any source. The only setback is losing coins to Snowbullies, plus the soft lock of having no nets and no money, which the math rooms fix.

### Cited Findings
- **Winning a climb [MULTI]:** A stage is won by putting all treasures in the castle chest at the top, which earns a prize. The player can then start again from the bottom ([Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Treasure_MathStorm!)). Each level ends with a prize that the reviewer felt gave a sense of achievement ([Potsdam](https://people.potsdam.edu/betrusak/reviews/readerrabbittreasuremath.html)).
- **Ranks [SINGLE primary]:**
  - Finding treasure raises the player's rank. Every few treasures earns a star, starting from "Super Seeker", then "1-star Super Seeker", and so on up to "Champion Super Seeker".
  - The rank messages say each step needs 2 more treasures. The clubhouse shows rank-up messages or says how many treasures are still needed.
  - Source: [Free Game Empire](https://www.freegameempire.com/games/Super-Solvers-Treasure-MathStorm/manual).
- **Difficulty rises with rank [MULTI]:** Higher ranks bring more treasures, harder riddles, and Snowbullies that steal money ([Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Treasure_MathStorm!)). Puzzles get harder over time ([Vizzed](https://www.vizzed.com/boards/thread.php?id=105443)). After a deposit the player can move to a harder skill level ([Archive.org TMS_VGA](https://archive.org/details/TMS_VGA)).
- **Snowbully ramp [MULTI]:** Snowbullies appear only in later climbs, start at one per level, get faster, and eventually come several at once ([Vizzed](https://www.vizzed.com/boards/thread.php?id=105443)). The LP counted up to two per level ([LP 16](https://lparchive.org/Super-Solvers-Series/Update%2016/)).
- **Final ending [SINGLE + partial corroboration]:** Once all the treasure is collected, the crown melts, the villain's snow machine breaks, and the mountain returns to spring or summer ([Vizzed](https://www.vizzed.com/boards/thread.php?id=105443)). The LP confirms a separate ending video exists ([LP 16](https://lparchive.org/Super-Solvers-Series/Update%2016/)). The artist confirms summer mountain art exists ([sk8nbike](https://sk8nbike.com/Treasure_Mathstorm_01.htm)).
  - **Conflict:** Wikipedia does not describe a final ending and presents the game as repeating climbs ([Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Treasure_MathStorm!)).
- **No penalty for wrong answers [MULTI]:** The time elf only shakes his head ([LP 14](https://lparchive.org/Super-Solvers-Series/Update%2014/)). ClassicReload says feedback is gentle and retries carry no penalty ([ClassicReload Win3.x](https://classicreload.com/win3x-treasure-mathstorm.html)), but that site is low-reliability.
- **Soft fail state [SINGLE primary]:** One hint covers running out of both nets and money, and points the player to earn money in the Time Igloo, the Gold Room or the Crystal Cave ([Free Game Empire](https://www.freegameempire.com/games/Super-Solvers-Treasure-MathStorm/manual)).
- **Play length [SINGLE]:** The reviewer estimated about 2 hours of play ([Potsdam](https://people.potsdam.edu/betrusak/reviews/readerrabbittreasuremath.html)).

### Inferences
- **Progression is a spiral.** The child replays the same three-level route many times, and each pass is harder through more treasures, harder math, higher prices and faster hazards. The growing toy shelf in the clubhouse is the visible record of progress.
- With no timer and no lives, the game has no "game over". Challenge comes from searching (which pile?), from managing resources (nets and coins), and from the math itself.

### Gaps
- The exact number of ranks is unknown, as are the treasures needed for each, and what "Champion Super Seeker" needs in total.
- Whether rank depends on total treasures deposited (implied) or on completed climbs is unconfirmed.
- Exactly when the final ending triggers is unconfirmed. Whether the game continues after the ending is also unknown.
- Whether difficulty adapts automatically or is chosen by the player is unclear. A search snippet for the Higher Intellect wiki claimed "six math levels that automatically adjust", but the page returned 403 and could not be verified ([Higher Intellect](https://wiki.preterhuman.net/Treasure_MathStorm!)). The LP mentions an "easier setting" in the clock room ([LP 14](https://lparchive.org/Super-Solvers-Series/Update%2014/)). A Retro Replay snippet named three skill levels, "Recover/Explore/Adventure" ([Retro Replay](https://retro-replay.com/db/dos/treasure-mathstorm/)). This looks unreliable or invented and is **not** supported by any other source.

## 6. Side activities and mini-games

### Takeaway
There are four structured math "rooms" and one treasure-hunt mechanic, plus the store, which works as a money-counting activity. Each room gives the gate tool for its level, then becomes a place to earn money.

| Location | Level | Skill | Reward | Sources |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Elves plus snowball piles | All levels | Arithmetic (problem gives a number), then adjusting a quantity | Coins; the special number, which leads to a treasure | Wikipedia, Free Game Empire, Vizzed, LP 14 |
| Time Igloo | Level 1 | Telling time: analog/digital matching, and ±15 minutes at higher ranks | 4 picks, then coins | Vizzed, LP 14, Free Game Empire |
| Gold Room (scale) | Level 2 | Equality and balancing; Mystery Bag (unknown weight) | 4 catapult parts, then coins | Vizzed, LP 15/16, Free Game Empire |
| Crystal Cave | Level 3 | Counting and place value (1s, 10s, 100s) | 4 ladders, then coins | Vizzed, LP 15/16, Free Game Empire |
| Store (on each level) | All levels | Paying exact change with coins, making change | Nets and tool parts | Free Game Empire, LP 14, Vizzed |
| Yeti gate | Level 3 top | Number patterns, missing number | Entry to the castle | Vizzed, LP 15 |
| Castle ladders | Summit | Choosing the correct ladder (rule unknown) | Reaching the chest | LP 15 |
| Prize room | Clubhouse | No math; click toys to animate them | Collection and pride | LP 14, Wikipedia |

### Cited Findings
- Supplies are earned by helping locals with math tasks such as setting clocks, balancing scales and counting crystals ([Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Treasure_MathStorm!)). Each row of the table above is cited in sections 3 and 4.
- The game uses classical music, notably Bach's Invention No. 8 ([Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Treasure_MathStorm!)).
- **No scoreboard or hall of fame** appears in any source consulted. Progress is shown through the clubhouse prize display and rank messages ([Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Treasure_MathStorm!); [Free Game Empire](https://www.freegameempire.com/games/Super-Solvers-Treasure-MathStorm/manual)).

### Inferences
- The "money" skill is not a separate mini-game. It is built into the store's exact-change rule, and the money earned from the other rooms feeds it.
- Each room has a rest state after the 4 required parts are earned: it keeps paying coins. This lets a child practise a skill they like as much as they want.

### Gaps
- No evidence was found of a parent or teacher options screen (for example, one that turns individual skills on or off), a printable report, or a score table. This is not confirmed either way.

## 7. Controls, save system, multiple players, and version differences

### Takeaway
- **DOS:** keyboard only. Arrows walk, down plus an arrow skates (and ducks), the spacebar swings the net, and Enter checks piles and answers.
- **Later CD versions (Windows/Mac):** add mouse support (dragging weights, clicking on-screen buttons) and spoken instructions.
- **Players:** the player types a name at the start. Per-player saving and a multi-name roster are likely but not confirmed.
- **Versions:** DOS 1992 in several graphics/sound variants, then Windows/Mac CD 1994, v2.0 for Windows/Mac in 1996–97, a UK v1.1 using pence, plus Spanish and Taiwanese editions. The Mac walkthrough found differences in character colours, voices, and the ending animation.

### Cited Findings
- **DOS keys [SINGLE]:** Left/Right arrow to walk, Down plus Left or Right to speed-skate, spacebar for the net, Enter to check answers and snowball piles ([ClassicReload DOS](https://classicreload.com/treasure-mathstorm.html)).
- **Other actions [MULTI]:** The in-game hint strings describe separate actions for:
  - swinging the net or catching an elf
  - adding snowballs to a pile
  - checking a pile
  - climbing or jumping at obstacles
  - adjusting values in the math rooms
  - confirming answers
  - placing coins on the store counter and getting change
  - The actual key names are placeholders in that text ([Free Game Empire](https://www.freegameempire.com/games/Super-Solvers-Treasure-MathStorm/manual)).
  - The player presses Enter to dismiss Chirp's messages ([Free Game Empire](https://www.freegameempire.com/games/Super-Solvers-Treasure-MathStorm/manual)).
  - Ducking is a separate move ([LP 16](https://lparchive.org/Super-Solvers-Series/Update%2016/); [Vizzed](https://www.vizzed.com/boards/thread.php?id=105443)).
- **Mouse [SINGLE]:** The mouse can drag weights in the Gold Room, and red buttons in the bottom-left corner control the whole game ([LP 15](https://lparchive.org/Super-Solvers-Series/Update%2015/)). The version is not stated, but it is probably a Windows CD release. In the CD version, children click to start each station's task ([Potsdam](https://people.potsdam.edu/betrusak/reviews/readerrabbittreasuremath.html)).
- **Intro and name entry [SINGLE]:** The intro can be skipped with the spacebar or a mouse click, and the player then types their name ([Vizzed](https://www.vizzed.com/boards/thread.php?id=105443)).
- **Single player [SINGLE]:** The reviewer lists the CD version as single-player ([Potsdam](https://people.potsdam.edu/betrusak/reviews/readerrabbittreasuremath.html)).
- **Versions [MULTI]:**

- **Version differences in play [SINGLE]:** The Mac walkthrough author says the versions differ mainly in character colours and voices, and that the ending differs: the villain is either frozen or blown over by snow ([Vizzed](https://www.vizzed.com/boards/thread.php?id=105443)).
- **Relation to the predecessor [SINGLE]:** The basic gameplay is very close to Treasure Mountain!, but this game runs more smoothly and looks better ([Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Treasure_MathStorm!)).

The descriptions in these notes come from the LP's narration, not from viewing the images.

### Inferences
- Within a climb, the controls are essentially one-axis platforming (left/right, duck/skate) plus one action button (net) and one confirm button. This is a very low motor-skill demand, suitable for ages 5–8.
- Typing the name at the start probably keys a saved profile, since the progress (rank, prizes) would be pointless without saving. This is inferred, not confirmed.

### Gaps
- **Saving.** I could not confirm how progress is saved: automatically by name, whether several children's names can be kept in one roster, or whether there is a delete-player option.
- **Options menu.** I could not confirm a settings or options menu (for sound, a skill picker, or a parent area). ClassicReload's "Esc pauses or opens settings" comes from a low-reliability fan section ([ClassicReload Win3.x](https://classicreload.com/win3x-treasure-mathstorm.html)).
- **Mechanical differences between DOS and CD versions.** None beyond voices and mouse support were documented.
- **Primary sources.** MobyGames, GameFAQs and the printed manual could not be reached (403 or not online). They would likely settle the gaps above: rank list, prices, options, save system and scroll-problem format.
